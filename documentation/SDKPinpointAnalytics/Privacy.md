# Privacy implications

Please be advised that, if you choose to enable **_AdswizzSDKPinpointAnalytics_**, you will have to inform your customers that your application handles and processes anonymized personal data. <br>
**We highly encourage you to loop your product and legal teams in the process of defining the best way to reflect this in your application’s Privacy Policy.**

---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
| Data accessed and collected for <br>**_AWS Pinpoint_** Analytics | Data accessed and collected for <br>**_AWS Pinpoint_** Demographics |
|:-----------------|:---------------------|
| * device make <br>* device model and locale <br>* device model version <br>* timezone <br>* app version <br>* app title <br>* platform <br>* platform version <br>* client SDK version <br>* app package name <br> | * address and location <br>* latitude <br>* longitude <br>* postal code <br>* city <br>* region <br>* country <br> |

> **Note**
> Data which refers to Demographics is retrieved if the location permission is granted and the reverse geocoding is successfull.
